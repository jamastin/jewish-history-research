United States

Slavery in the United States runs straight into the Civil War section — before the war, during it, and after it — and items will be cross-filed here as they are gathered. The first cross-filed item is a newspaper passage about the Southern planter economy:

Album item M071 — a newspaper page (Library of Congress; the exact paper and date are still being hunted).

"The desperate condition of the white planters of the south shows us this: they are the slaves of the Jews, who control the money market. So in turn the black laborers are the slaves of the white planters."

— Album item M071; also filed under Rothschilds & Money Schemes and in the Civil War section, Before the War
