In their own words — key quotations

"Baron Hirsch, the wealthy associate of the Prince of Wales, has telegraphed Jesse Seligman, treasurer of the Baron Hirsch Fund, that the trustees of the fund may draw upon him for $2,500,000, to be applied to the relief of the Hebrew immigrants to this country."

— from: Baron Hirsch — Chronicling America finds (Library of Congress)

"We do not only give them the money they need to start afresh in this country, but we buy farms, tools, and everything that is necessary to put them on their feet, so that they can make a good and independent living."

— from: Baron Hirsch — Chronicling America finds (Library of Congress)

"The project is one of surpassing magnitude and of an extraordinary character."

— from: Baron Hirsch — Chronicling America finds (Library of Congress)

"From the fact, however, that he has purchased broad tracts of land in the Argentine Republic, and other tracts in South Africa, and has disbursed great sums of money for the service of Jewish immigrants to the United States…"

— from: Baron Hirsch — Chronicling America finds (Library of Congress)

"Vienna, April 21.—Baron Hirsch died this morning of heart disease at his estate near Komorn"

— from: Baron Hirsch — Chronicling America finds (Library of Congress)
