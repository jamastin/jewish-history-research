In their own words — key quotations

"Slavery is likely to be abolished by the war power and chattel slavery destroyed. This, I and my European friends are glad of, for slavery is but the owning of labor and carries with it the care of the laborers, while the European plan, led by England, is that capital shall control labor by controlling wages. This can be done by controlling the money. The great debt that capitalists will see to it is made out of the war, must be used as a means to control the volume of money. To accomplish this, the bonds must be used as a banking basis. … It will not do to allow the greenback, as it is called, to circulate as money any length of time, as we cannot control that."

— from: The Hazard Circular (July 1862) — chattel slavery to debt slavery, and the greenback

"In 1917, the international and imported troublemakers from America and Hungary — Trotzky and Bela Kun, to be specific — went to Russia. Their lips dripped with the same promise made by Earl Browder in New York last week… One hundred and fifty million Greek Catholics believed these internationalists. The result was wholesale murder, rape and destruction… Internationalists poured into Spain nine years ago. Their deeds of the past speak more eloquently than their promises of the present."

— from: Father Coughlin — "imported" communists and Russia

"The C.I.O., under the inspiration of Communist leadership, has provoked labor warfare. It imported the sit-down strike from Russia, and used it to terrorize workers and employers… Communist membership and leadership infiltrates the C.I.O. There can be no denying this."

— from: Father Coughlin — "imported" communists and Russia

"In 1936, these internationalists set up Col. David Toro as president to head a socialist state under imported Moscow Communism. In July, 1937, a bloodless coup led by the 33-year-old Lieut. Col. Busch threw the Left out."

— from: Coughlin's *Social Justice* — "Imported" Communists / Communism: Full-Search Results

"For years the anthracite coal operators imported thousands of poor immigrants from the eastern countries of Europe as cheap labor, and created this exploitation [which] left the area an easy prey to the penetrating poison which emanates from Moscow."

— from: Coughlin's *Social Justice* — "Imported" Communists / Communism: Full-Search Results
