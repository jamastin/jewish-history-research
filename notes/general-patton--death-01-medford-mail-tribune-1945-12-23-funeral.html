Patton's Death — Medford Mail Tribune, December 23, 1945

  • Newspaper: Medford Mail Tribune — Medford, Oregon
  • Date: December 23, 1945 — Page 1
  • Headline: "Widow Selects Third Army Cemetery At Hamm — Funeral Rites Monday"
  • Link: https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn97071090/1945-12-23/ed-1/seq-1/ocr/
  • Source: Historic Oregon Newspapers (University of Oregon; Library of Congress newspaper catalog, LCCN sn97071090)

In the paper's own words

"Heidelberg, Dec. 22 (U.P.) — Gen. George S. Patton's sorrowing old comrades in arms mustered in this holiday-decked German town today to escort him on Christmas eve to his last bivouac [in] Luxembourg, the great burial ground of his beloved Third Army."

"Patton's body lay in state from six until 10 p.m. tonight in the picturesque Villa Reiner adjoining U.S. Army headquarters on a hilltop overlooking Heidelberg and the Neckar River."

Also on the page: "Gen. George S. Patton's death has not changed the Army's decision not to prosecute either of the two military chauffeurs involved in the accident."
