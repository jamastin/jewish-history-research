The Historians' Totals, Compared

The totals below were produced independently — different researchers, different decades, different methods (documents, demography, or both). They do not copy each other, and they land in the same range. That convergence is the point.

Who When Total Jewish victims Basis
International Military Tribunal, Nuremberg 1945–46 5.7 million Captured German documents and demographic evidence presented by the prosecution
Raul Hilberg, The Destruction of the European Jews 1961 ~5.1 million Built bottom-up from German records, country by country; deliberately conservative — he counted only what documents could carry
Lucy Dawidowicz, The War Against the Jews 1975 ~5.93 million (5,933,900) Demographic reconstruction, country by country
US Holocaust Memorial Museum (current) ongoing ~5.75–5.95 million, stated as "six million" Sum of documented categories (below)

How the USHMM total is assembled (its published breakdown)

Category Jewish victims
Killing centers (Chełmno, Bełżec, Sobibór, Treblinka, Auschwitz-Birkenau) ~2.7 million
Mass shooting operations and related massacres ~2 million
Ghettos, labor camps and concentration camps (privation, disease, brutality) 800,000–1,000,000
Other acts of violence outside camps and ghettos (pogroms, death marches, individual killings) at least 250,000

Killing-center detail from the same USHMM table: Chełmno at least 152,000; Bełżec approximately 435,000 (compare Höfle's 434,508 arrivals for 1942 alone); Sobibór at least 167,000; Treblinka approximately 925,000; Auschwitz complex approximately 1,000,000.

Source: USHMM, "Documenting Numbers of Victims of the Holocaust and Nazi Persecution": https://encyclopedia.ushmm.org/content/en/article/documenting-numbers-of-victims-of-the-holocaust-and-nazi-persecution

Why the range is a range — honestly

  • Hilberg's 5.1 million is the floor: he excluded categories he could not document to his own standard (for example, many deaths in Romanian-controlled territory and some ghetto mortality).
  • The differences between 5.1 and 5.95 million are arguments about specific categories and border definitions, conducted in the open, in print, for sixty years. Nobody's total is anywhere near one or two million, and no serious demographic reconstruction can make the missing millions appear alive anywhere.
  • "Six million" is the rounded midpoint of the documented range — the same way the Korherr Report (the SS's own count, March 1943, with two years of killing still to go) already recorded a decline of ~4 million by the start of 1943.

The names check

Yad Vashem's Central Database of Shoah Victims' Names has identified more than 4.5 million victims by name — approaching five million — each with a documentary trail (a Page of Testimony, a deportation list, a camp record). A fabricated total could not survive a name-by-name database of that size; the database is the total, one person at a time, still being completed.
