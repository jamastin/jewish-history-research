The "Jews killed Hitler's cousin in Austria" story — checked

Hartheim Castle, Austria — a Nazi euthanasia killing centre. Source: Wikimedia Commons

Researched 2026-11-21. The half-remembered story is dug into here. This file checks every real story that could be behind the half-remembered story, against the record, and adds what the newspapers of the time (and since) actually printed.

The short answer

No record exists of Jews killing a cousin of Hitler in Austria — or anywhere else. But a real cousin of Hitler was killed in Austria: Aloisia Veit, gassed on 6 December 1940 at the Hartheim killing centre near Linz. Her killers were not Jews. She was murdered by Hitler's own regime, under its "Aktion T4" programme for killing psychiatric patients. The most likely explanation of the story is that the Veit story reached him with the killer flipped — or blended with one of the two famous cases in which a Jewish man really did shoot a Nazi official (Davos 1936 and Paris 1938, both detailed below; neither victim was a relative of Hitler, and neither place was Austria).

Story 1 — Aloisia Veit: the cousin who really was killed in Austria

Who she was. Aloisia Veit (born 18 July 1891, Pontafel, Austria-Hungary; died 6 December 1940, Alkoven, Upper Austria). She was related to Hitler through his father's Schicklgruber line: her great-grandmother Josefa Schicklgruber was the sister of Hitler's grandmother Maria Anna Schicklgruber. Sources describe the relationship as second cousin once removed (German reference works call her a "Nichte 3. Grades"). She worked as a housemaid/chambermaid.

Her illness and confinement. Her Vienna patient file records "schizophrene Geistesstörung, mit Ratlosigkeit und Depression, Zerfahrenheit, Sinnestäuschungen und Wahnideen" ("schizophrenic mental disorder, with perplexity and depression, incoherence, sensory delusions and delusions"). She told doctors she was haunted by ghosts and saw a skull; she spent much of her time chained to an iron bed, and in one letter begged to be given poison to end her suffering. She was admitted to the Am Steinhof asylum in Vienna on 26 January 1932 and spent nine years in closed wards.

How she died. On 28 November 1940 she was transferred to the municipal sanatorium at Ybbs an der Donau, and on 6 December 1940 she was deported from there to the NS-Tötungsanstalt Hartheim — Hartheim Castle at Alkoven, near Linz, Austria — and murdered in its gas chamber (carbon monoxide). She was 49. Hartheim was one of the six killing centres of Aktion T4, the Nazi programme, authorised by Hitler personally (his written authorisation is dated 1 September 1939), under which doctors murdered people deemed mentally or physically unfit. The regime's own "Hartheim statistics," found by US investigators in a safe at the castle in June 1945, record 18,269 people gassed there between May 1940 and 1 September 1941 alone; the total for Hartheim is estimated at about 30,000. The doctors who ran Hartheim were Nazi physicians Rudolf Lonauer and Georg Renno. There is no Jewish involvement in her death in any source — the selection, transport and killing were carried out entirely by the Nazi medical and SS apparatus.

How the story surfaced. The killing was secret at the time. The documents were recovered after the war (a file copy reached the US via papers recovered by a US Army private, Eric Hamm; David Irving had mentioned the case in print as early as the 1991 edition of his Hitler's War). The modern research is by American historian Timothy W. Ryback and German researcher Florian M. Beierl, working with Munich forensic specialist Wolfgang Eisenmenger from August 2004; they published their findings in January 2005. A scholarly chapter followed: Annette Hinz-Wessels, "Aloisia Veit – Ein 'Euthanasie'-Opfer aus Hitlers Familie," in „Das Vergessen der Vernichtung ist Teil der Vernichtung selbst" (Wallstein, 2007). A secret Gestapo report of 1944 had labelled her branch of the family "idiotic progeny." Whether Hitler personally knew of her fate is not established — Ryback and Beierl said so plainly.

One softer detail, flagged honestly: genealogical accounts (WikiTree) say Hitler's sister Paula attempted an intervention for Aloisia. This is repeated in retellings but is less firmly documented than the core facts above, so treat it as "reported," not proven.

Sources: - German Wikipedia, "Aloisia Veit" (built on Hinz-Wessels 2007 and Neumann & Eberle, War Hitler krank?, 2009): https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aloisia_Veit - Wikipedia, "Hartheim killing centre" (lists Aloisia Veit among the well-known victims; gives the Hartheim statistics and the T4 doctors): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hartheim_Euthanasia_Centre - Kate Connolly, The Daily Telegraph, 19 January 2005 (see the newspaper section below): reprinted at History News Network, https://www.hnn.us/article/hitlers-mentally-ill-cousin-killed-in-nazi-gas-cha - WikiTree profile (genealogy, with the Paula detail flagged above): https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Veit-204

Story 2 — The Schmidt cousins: Hitler relatives arrested in Austria who died in custody — but Soviet custody

In May 1945, Soviet forces in Austria arrested five of Hitler's relatives: his first cousins Maria, Johann and Eduard Schmidt, Maria's husband Ignaz Koppensteiner, and Johann Schmidt Jr. Koppensteiner — arrested on the stated ground that he "approved of [Hitler's] criminal plans against the USSR" — died in a Moscow prison in 1949. Eduard Schmidt died in Soviet custody in 1951 and Maria Schmidt in 1953. Johann Jr. was released in 1955. Russia posthumously pardoned them all in 1997.

So this is a second real "Hitler's cousins died after arrest in Austria" story — but the killers were the Soviet security organs, not Jews.

Why it matters here: a narrated slideshow video about "what happened to Hitler's relatives," posted on Facebook on 17 November 2026 (days before this), retells the Schmidt story with errors — it says Johann Schmidt was taken to Vienna and "died in custody," which compresses and garbles the record above. Videos of exactly this kind are a plausible channel by which a blurred "cousin killed in Austria" memory forms. (Link: https://www.facebook.com/reel/1799188558051055/ — a low-quality social-media retelling; treat as an example of the garbling process, not as a source.)

Source: Wikipedia, "Family of Adolf Hitler" (Soviet arrests and deaths in custody; posthumous pardons, 1997): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Family_of_Adolf_Hitler

Story 3 — David Frankfurter and Wilhelm Gustloff: a Jew really did shoot a Nazi leader — in Switzerland, 1936

The facts. David Frankfurter (1909–1982), a Yugoslav Jewish medical student from Croatia, the son of a rabbi, walked into the Davos home of Wilhelm Gustloff on 4 February 1936 and shot him five times, killing him. Gustloff was the founder and leader of the Swiss branch of the Nazi Party (NSDAP/AO) and had promoted distribution of the Protocols of the Elders of Zion in Switzerland. Frankfurter surrendered at once and told police, in the words repeated in every account: "I fired the shots because I am a Jew." He was tried at Chur in December 1936, convicted of murder and sentenced to 18 years; he was pardoned and released in 1945 and settled in Israel.

Why it could feed the story: Davos is an Alpine resort town, easily misremembered as Austria; Gustloff was a senior Nazi, easily upgraded in retelling to someone close to Hitler. But Gustloff was no relation to Hitler at all, and Davos is in Switzerland.

The propaganda angle. Goebbels turned Gustloff into a Nazi "blood martyr" (Blutzeuge); a state funeral followed, and the party publishing house issued Wolfgang Diewerge's 1937 booklet Ein Jude hat geschossen ("A Jew Has Shot") to frame the lone assassin as the agent of "world Jewry." This is the era's most heavily propagandised "a Jew killed a Nazi" story — exactly the kind that survives in retellings for 90 years.

Sources: - Yad Vashem, Shoah Resource Center, "Frankfurter, David": https://wwv.yadvashem.org/odot_pdf/Microsoft%20Word%20-%205862.pdf - US Holocaust Memorial Museum, trial records collection: https://collections.ushmm.org/search/catalog/irn47979 - Wikipedia, "David Frankfurter": https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Frankfurter

Story 4 — Herschel Grynszpan and Ernst vom Rath: Paris, 1938 — the pretext for Kristallnacht

The facts. Herschel Grynszpan (1921 – fate unknown after 1945), a 17-year-old Polish-Jewish refugee in Paris, learned that his parents had been expelled from Germany to the Polish border in the mass deportation of late October 1938. On the morning of 7 November 1938 he went to the German embassy in Paris and shot the first official shown to him, Third Secretary Ernst vom Rath (1909–1938), who died of his wounds on 9 November. Grynszpan said he acted to avenge the persecuted Jews. The Nazi leadership used the shooting that same night as the pretext for Kristallnacht (9–10 November 1938), the nationwide pogrom.

Why it could feed the story: it is the other world-famous "young Jew shoots a German official" story, and Kristallnacht raged across Austria too (Vienna's synagogues were destroyed), which can attach "Austria" to the memory. But vom Rath was a junior diplomat, not a relative of Hitler, and the shooting was in Paris.

Sources: - Yad Vashem, Shoah Resource Center, "Grynszpan, Herschel": https://wwv.yadvashem.org/odot_pdf/Microsoft%20Word%20-%206321.pdf - Smithsonian Magazine, "How a Jewish Teenager Went From Refugee to Assassin to Puppet of Nazi Propaganda": https://www.smithsonianmag.com/history/how-teenaged-jewish-boy-went-refugee-assassin-puppet-nazi-propaganda-180971204/ - Wikipedia, "Ernst vom Rath": https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ernst_vom_Rath

Completeness sweep — every other violent death in Hitler's family

To be thorough, the rest of the family's violent deaths were checked. None involves a Jewish killer:

  • Geli Raubal (1908–1931), Hitler's half-niece: found shot dead in Hitler's Munich apartment on 19 September 1931, with Hitler's pistol; officially suicide. Rumours at the time pointed at Hitler himself, not at any Jew — and it was Munich, not Austria. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geli_Raubal
  • Heinz Hitler (1920–1942), Hitler's nephew: captured by Soviet forces on the Eastern Front on 10 January 1942 and died in Moscow's Butyrka prison, aged 21, after interrogation and torture. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Family_of_Adolf_Hitler
  • The Schmidt cousins and Ignaz Koppensteiner (Story 2): Soviet custody.

The one real Jewish connection to a Hitler family death in Austria runs the opposite way. Dr Eduard Bloch, a Jewish physician in Linz, treated Hitler's mother Klara through her final illness in 1907, charging the poor family little or nothing; the young Hitler sent him thank-you postcards ("I shall be grateful to you forever"). After the Anschluss, Hitler personally had Bloch placed under Gestapo protection, called him an Edeljude ("noble Jew"), and let him emigrate to the United States in 1940. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eduard_Bloch ; scholarly version: George M. Weisz, "Hitler's Jewish Physicians," Rambam Maimonides Medical Journal 2014: https://www.rmmj.org.il/issues/23/411/manuscript

What the newspapers said — the press record

On Veit / Hartheim

There is no wartime newspaper coverage of Aloisia Veit's death: Aktion T4 was a secret programme and her killing in December 1940 was never reported at the time. The story entered the newspapers only when Ryback and Beierl published in January 2005:

  • The Daily Telegraph (London), 19 January 2005, Kate Connolly in Berlin, headline "Hitler's mentally ill cousin 'killed in Nazi gas chamber'." Exact opening words: "Adolf Hitler's cousin was gassed under the Nazi policy of eliminating mental health patients, according to recently discovered documents." (The Telegraph text is archived/paywalled; the article is reprinted in full at History News Network: https://www.hnn.us/article/hitlers-mentally-ill-cousin-killed-in-nazi-gas-cha — note that a copy also circulates on David Irving's Focal Point website; Irving is a convicted Holocaust denier, so that site is a fringe host and is flagged as such — the underlying Telegraph article is genuine.)
  • Focus Online (Germany), 17 January 2005: "Hitler: 'Geisteskranke' Cousine starb in Gaskammer" ("Hitler: 'mentally ill' cousin died in gas chamber") — the German news announcement of the same findings (cited in the German Wikipedia article on Veit).
  • The Independent (London), January 2005, headline "Hitler's mentally ill relative was sent to gas chamber," reporting she "was gassed to death at Hartheim Castle near the northern Austrian city of Linz on 6 December 1940" (catalogue record: https://www.mbrl.ae/web/guest/asset-details?documentId=summon____FETCH-proquest_newspapers_3372576153).

Verdict — where the story most likely comes from

The story has three parts: (a) a cousin of Hitler, (b) killed, (c) in Austria. Only one real story matches all three: Aloisia Veit. She really was Hitler's cousin, she really was killed, and it really happened in Austria (Hartheim, near Linz). What got changed in the retelling is the identity of the killer: in the record, she was murdered by the Nazi regime's own euthanasia programme — the machinery of her famous cousin — whereas in the version in circulation, "Jews" did the killing.

Two blends could have helped the flip along:

  1. The Frankfurter (1936) and Grynszpan (1938) stories are the two most famous "a Jew shot a Nazi" cases of the era, both blasted across the world's newspapers and both milked by Nazi propaganda as proof of a Jewish world conspiracy. Either could supply the "Jews killing" element to a half-remembered story — and Davos (Alpine, German-speaking) is easily misremembered as Austria.
  2. Social-media retellings of Hitler-family stories are actively garbling these facts right now — the November 2026 Facebook slideshow on the Schmidt cousins (Story 2) already misstates who died where. A version of the Veit story with the killer reversed would fit the same ecosystem. Honest gap: no published version stating in so many words that "Jews killed Hitler's cousin" was found in tthe search — the flip may have happened in a video narration, a comment thread, or in memory itself. What can be said flatly is that no such event exists in the historical record.

Honest gaps

  • Whether Hitler knew of Aloisia Veit's illness or death: unproven either way (Ryback and Beierl state this themselves). A 1944 Gestapo report on the family exists; no document shows Hitler intervening — or ordering her death specifically. She died because the T4 machinery processed her like any other patient.
  • The "Paula tried to intervene" detail rests on genealogical retelling, not on the scholarly chapter; flagged where it appears above.
  • Herschel Grynszpan's own fate after 1940 is unknown (he was handed to the Gestapo; declared dead in 1960; a 2016 claim that he survived the war surfaced in a photograph identification but remains disputed).
  • Exact wording of some 1930s headlines is quoted from catalogues/reference lists where the original page image is paywalled (Newspapers.com / NYT archive); each such case is noted next to the quote. The Oregon Statesman and Detroit Jewish News quotes are from the papers' own scanned pages.
