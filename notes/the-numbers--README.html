The Numbers — Nazi Records & Population Counts

What this folder is: a records check, done on 2026-11-21, prompted by a Facebook video questioning the Holocaust death toll. Instead of arguing about the video, this folder goes to the underlying evidence itself — the Nazi regime's own paperwork and the before-and-after population counts — and shows, document by document, how the total of roughly six million murdered Jews is actually built. Every figure below is quoted from the record named, with a tappable link to the document or archive page itself.

The short answer first: there is no single document that says "six million." The number is a total built the way you would build any total — by adding up separate, independent piles of evidence that were created by different people, in different places, for different purposes, and that agree with each other:

  1. The killers' own running tallies — the Korherr Report and the Höfle Telegram (two separate SS documents that match each other to the exact digit: 1,274,166).
  2. The killing units' own reports — the Einsatzgruppen situation reports, e.g. the Jäger Report's itemized 137,346 from one unit in five months.
  3. Camp-by-camp reconstruction from transport records — e.g. Auschwitz: about 1.3 million deported in, about 1.1 million murdered, rebuilt train by train.
  4. The population counts — about 9.5 million Jews in Europe in 1933; about 3.5 million in 1950. The missing millions did not emigrate anywhere that shows up in any country's records.
  5. The historians' independent totals — Hilberg ~5.1 million, Dawidowicz ~5.93 million, the Nuremberg tribunal 5.7 million, the US Holocaust Memorial Museum's reconstruction ~5.75–5.95 million. Different methods, same range.

The files in this folder

  • korherr-report.md — the SS's own statistical report to Himmler (March 1943): 1,873,549 "evacuations" and a stated decline of ~4 million Jews in Europe by the start of 1943.
  • hofle-telegram.md — the intercepted SS radio telegram (January 11, 1943): 1,274,166 arrivals at the four Operation Reinhard camps in 1942 alone.
  • einsatzgruppen-reports.md — the mobile killing squads' reports; the Jäger Report itemizes 137,346 murders in Lithuania in five months.
  • auschwitz-numbers.md — how the Auschwitz figure was rebuilt from transport records (~1.1 million), what the camp's death books do and don't cover, and the true story of the Soviet "4 million" figure and the plaque.
  • population-counts.md — the demographic check: Jewish population by country, 1933 vs after the war.
  • historians-totals.md — the major totals side by side, and Yad Vashem's ~5 million victims identified by name.
  • the-full-picture.md — the whole paper trail: the laws, the decision documents, the trains and the billing, the camp files, where the records live today, what the Nazis destroyed, and how the documents were used after the war.

Honesty notes

  • Figures are given exactly as the sources give them. Where sources differ, the range is shown and the difference is explained, not smoothed over.
  • The perpetrators wrote in euphemisms — "evacuation," "special treatment," "resettlement." The files flag each one where it appears.
  • Some records were destroyed by the Nazis in 1943–45 (see the-full-picture.md). What survives is a floor, not a ceiling: the documents that remain are what the totals are built from, and gaps are stated where they exist.
