General Patton — The Daily News Editorial Apology (as carried in The Sentinel)

Found on 2026-11-18: the piece mentioned in the Der Tog editorial (article #8) — the long-awaited New York Daily News editorial apology for John O'Donnell's column charging a Jewish plot over General Patton's removal.

  • Newspaper carrying it: The Sentinel — Chicago, Ill.
  • Date: October 25, 1945 (Vol. 140, Issue 4)
  • Headline: "O'DONNELL RETRACTS FALSEHOOD! Daily News Offers Apology For Slur On Jews"
  • Link: http://www.idaillinois.org/digital/api/collection/p16614coll14/id/54988/download
  • Also carried in: The Jewish Post, Oct 19, 1945 — https://newspapers.library.in.gov/?a=is&oid=JPOST19451019-01.1.5&type=staticpdf& ; and The Detroit Jewish News, Oct 26, 1945 — https://digital.bentley.umich.edu/djnews/djn.1945.10.26.001/20

The Daily News editorial, in its own words (as quoted in The Sentinel)

"That column," the News says, "was construed as anti-Semitic. The News has been asked to state its own position on the subject of anti-Semitism. Our position is what it has always been. The News is neither anti-Semitic nor pro-Semitic; neither anti-Catholic nor pro-Catholic; neither anti-Protestant nor pro-Protestant..."

The editorial further explains that "columnists are given more leeway in expressing their own views; more so than writers of the material that appears in the news columns. The editorial column, of course, expresses the views of the paper."

O'Donnell's retraction, in his own words (same article)

"On the evidence, our statements in Capitol Stuff were untrue. We regret having made them."

The Sentinel's lead: "Sixteen days after it was proven absolutely false, New York Daily News columnist John O'Donnell, retracted his allegation, made in his October 3 column, that pressure from influential American Jews caused the removal of General George S. Patton from command of the U.S. Third Army."

Note: The New York Daily News itself is not in Chronicling America, so its editorial apology survives in the papers that quoted it, like The Sentinel above.
