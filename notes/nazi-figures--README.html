The Other Major Figures

Subfolders for the major Nazi figures around Hitler, sitting alongside Hitler's own folder (hitler-writings-and-reading/, which stays his alone). Each subfolder is a starter page: who the man was, the part he played, and his key documents — with links to the actual sources and reliability flags where the record needs them.

  • heinrich-himmler/ — Reichsführer-SS; ran the SS and police empire that carried out the genocide. Key records: the Posen speeches, his service calendars, the Korherr correspondence, his May 1940 memorandum on the peoples of the East.
  • reinhard-heydrich/ — Himmler's deputy; head of the Reich Security Main Office (RSHA). Key records: the Wannsee Protocol, Göring's 31 July 1941 authorization to him, the Einsatzgruppen command structure.
  • adolf-eichmann/ — the RSHA's deportation-logistics chief (Section IV B 4). Key records: the 1961 Jerusalem trial record, the Bureau 06 pre-trial interrogation, and the Sassen interviews (flagged).
  • hermann-goring/ — Hitler's designated successor and head of the Four-Year Plan. Key record: his signed 31 July 1941 “total solution” authorization letter to Heydrich; lead defendant at Nuremberg.
  • joseph-goebbels/ — propaganda minister. Key records: his diaries (the Fröhlich critical edition) and the 18 February 1943 Sportpalast “total war” speech.
  • rudolf-hoss/ — commandant of Auschwitz. Key records: his Nuremberg affidavit (3868-PS), his testimony, and the memoir he wrote in Kraków in 1946–47 — all perpetrator testimony, flagged as such.

Each starter page also has an “In the papers” section (added 2026-11-21 at James's request): verified newspaper items about the man — American papers, British papers, and the Jewish press — with the paper's exact words, the paper and date, and a link to the actual scanned page where one exists. Paywalled or un-scanned items are flagged plainly, and thin sweeps (Höss's) are stated as such rather than padded. Hitler's own page has the same section in his folder's README.
