Patton's Death — Evening Star (Washington, D.C.), December 23–24, 1945

From the earlier General Patton search in Chronicling America; saved into this subfolder at James's request on 2026-11-18 with the other death coverage.

December 23, 1945 — Gen. Walker on Patton's death

  • Link: https://www.loc.gov/resource/sn83045462/1945-12-23/ed-1/?sp=1&q=Patton+death
  • Lt. Gen. Walton Walker, who succeeded Patton in command of the Fifteenth Army: "With the passing of General Patton, the Army loses one of the most brilliant and successful soldiers of our time."

December 24, 1945 — Patton buried

  • Link: https://www.loc.gov/resource/sn83045462/1945-12-24/ed-1/?sp=1&q=Patton+buried
  • Headline: "Patton Is Buried In Luxembourg" — Patton was buried on Christmas Eve at the American military cemetery at Hamm, Luxembourg, among the soldiers of his Third Army, as he had wished.

Also already on file (main "where did we go wrong" folder)

  • The Key West Citizen, December 21, 1945 — in patton-jews-1945.md: Patton died at 5:50 p.m. Heidelberg time, age 60; he had been injured December 9 at Mannheim.
