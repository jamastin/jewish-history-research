General Patton — Article 7: The Commentator

  • Newspaper: The Commentator — Jacksonville, Fla.
  • Date: October 25, 1945 — Image 1 (page 1)
  • Headline: "O'Donnell Apologizes, Retracting Falsehoods"
  • Link: https://www.loc.gov/resource/sn95026724/1945-10-25/ed-1/?sp=1&q=Patton+Jews
  • Source: Library of Congress, Chronicling America

Brief statement, in the paper's own words

"The soldier, Pfc. Charles H. Kuhl of Mishawaka, Ind., who was slapped by the general for a display of cowardice, is not a Jew, O'Donnell now said."

Also in the article: "Mr. O'Donnell now says that the statement that he had been told that General Patton was suffering from a venereal disease was untrue."
