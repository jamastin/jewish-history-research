Father Coughlin — "imported" communists and Russia

Search run 2026-11-19. Coughlin material sits in this folder alongside the other Social Justice finds.

The exact line in Coughlin's own paper

Social Justice, June 13, 1938 (Vol. 1A, No. 15), from the article material around "Communism — the Theory" / "Go to the Poor!":

"In 1917, the international and imported troublemakers from America and Hungary — Trotzky and Bela Kun, to be specific — went to Russia. Their lips dripped with the same promise made by Earl Browder in New York last week… One hundred and fifty million Greek Catholics believed these internationalists. The result was wholesale murder, rape and destruction… Internationalists poured into Spain nine years ago. Their deeds of the past speak more eloquently than their promises of the present."

Note the direction of the claim as Coughlin printed it: the "imported" troublemakers went into Russia (Trotsky from America, Bela Kun from Hungary). Full issue text: https://archive.org/download/socnew_1938a_vol01a_11_202602/socnew_1938a_vol01a_15_text.pdf

The other direction — imported FROM Russia

Social Justice, January 1, 1940, "Communism in C.I.O. — Labor's 1940 Challenge":

"The C.I.O., under the inspiration of Communist leadership, has provoked labor warfare. It imported the sit-down strike from Russia, and used it to terrorize workers and employers… Communist membership and leadership infiltrates the C.I.O. There can be no denying this."

Full issue text: https://archive.org/download/socnew_1940_vol05a_07/socnew_1940_vol05a_01_text.pdf

More publications on the same theme

  • The full run of Social Justice (1936–1942) is on archive.org (the "Padre Charles Coughlin" collection, socnew_* files). The communists-and-Russia theme runs through it constantly. Example: the September 11, 1939 issue serializes "Chapter Fourteen: Jewish Share in German Communism," defending Coughlin's claim that "in Germany, as in Russia, the atheistic and irreligious Jews played a large part in the Communist movement." https://archive.org/download/socnew_1938a_vol01a_11_202602/socnew_1939_vol04a_11_text.pdf
  • Social Justice, July 7, 1941 — front page "Christians Will Not Support New Red Deal," quoting Pius XI's encyclical: "Communism is intrinsically wrong, and no one who [wishes to save Christian civilization] may collaborate with it in any manner whatsoever." https://archive.org/download/socnew_1938a_vol01a_11_202602/socnew_1941_vol08_01_text.pdf
  • Coughlin's named source: Fr. Denis Fahey. After the November 20, 1938 Kristallnacht broadcast (where Coughlin claimed Jewish-led communists in Russia had murdered over 20 million Christians — figures taken from a Goebbels speech), Coughlin announced the next Sunday: "since I am forced to defend myself… let me introduce into court as my witness the scholarly Professor Denis Fahey." Fahey's book The Mystical Body of Christ in the Modern World (1935) was his cited "proof" that communism was a plot toward "a world-state in which the Jewish race will be supreme." Fahey's books are back in print (Loreto Publications reissues, 2018 onward).
  • The Protocols of the Elders of Zion — Coughlin serialized this in Social Justice in 1938; it is the Russian-origin forgery his paper used as documentary backing for the Jewish-communism claims.
  • Later commentary: PBS/JTA's "Radioactive: The Father Coughlin Story" summarizes that he used Social Justice "to share conspiracy theories about how the Jews were controlling communism, were controlling the world financial markets" — https://www.propwatch.org/article.php?id=338&t=1660

Honest note

What Coughlin printed most often is the claim that communism itself was a Jewish-run import — into Russia by men like Trotsky, and into America from Russia (tactics, organizers, the sit-down strike). For one specific passage where Coughlin names who "imported communists from Russia," the June 13, 1938 and January 1, 1940 issues above are the closest exact-text matches found so far; the archive.org full run can be searched issue by issue for more.
