Rudolf Höss (1901–1947)

Who he was: The first and longest-serving commandant of Auschwitz (May 1940 – December 1943), who built the camp and set up its extermination operation, and who returned in May 1944 to oversee the murder of Hungary's Jews. He was tried by Poland's Supreme National Tribunal in Warsaw in March 1947 and hanged at Auschwitz on 16 April 1947.

⚠ Everything below is perpetrator testimony. Höss is a uniquely detailed witness to how Auschwitz worked, and his accounts are corroborated on the machinery by documents and other evidence — but he wrote and spoke as a defendant, presented himself as an order-follower, and his numbers were not always reliable. Read him the way historians do: for the operation, checked against the record — never as the final word on the toll.

Key documents

1. The Nuremberg affidavit — 3868-PS, 5 April 1946

Höss's sworn affidavit describes running Auschwitz, the start of gassing in summer 1941, his visit to Treblinka, and the use of Zyklon B. Known caveat: its headline figure — “at least 2,500,000 victims… about 3,000,000” dead at Auschwitz — is far above the figure established by later research from transport records (about 1.1 million murdered there). Höss said himself he never knew the true total and had taken figures from Eichmann; the inflated affidavit number is the part of his testimony historians do not rely on. - Read the affidavit (Harvard Nuremberg Trials Project): https://nuremberg.law.harvard.edu/documents/1757-affidavit-concerning-auschwitz-concentration?mode=text

2. His Nuremberg testimony — 15 April 1946

Höss testified at the International Military Tribunal — called, strikingly, as a defence witness for Ernst Kaltenbrunner — and confirmed the affidavit's account of the extermination programme in open court.

3. The Kraków memoir — written 1946–47

In Polish custody, at the suggestion of investigating judge Jan Sehn, Höss wrote his autobiography and essays on the camp's operation (from November 1946 until his trial). The handwritten manuscript is held by the Auschwitz-Birkenau State Museum; it was published in German as Kommandant in Auschwitz (edited by Martin Broszat, 1958) and in English as Commandant of Auschwitz / Death Dealer. The Museum's own account of his trial notes that in both the memoir and the trial he “sought to portray himself primarily as someone who was simply carrying out orders” and pushed knowledge of the extermination toward others — the standing caution for everything he wrote. - Auschwitz-Birkenau State Museum — on Höss's trial and writings: https://www.auschwitz.org/en/education/e-learning/podcast/the-trial-of-rudolf-hss-and-other-ss-garrison/

In the papers

Newspaper coverage of Höss, verified 2026-11-21. His sweep is thinner than the others' — he was a camp commandant, not a public figure, and the papers mostly met him in 1946. What was actually verified is listed; nothing is padded.

  • The Detroit Jewish News, 29 March 1946, page 3 — days after his capture, the Jewish press reported his first confession (via British officials in Berlin): “Rudolf Hoess, 45-year-old commandant of Nazi extermination camps in southern Poland, confessed that he, personally, arranged for the gas murders of 2,000,000 persons, mostly Jews, at Oswiecim death camp, ‘on orders from Heinrich Himmler.’” ⚠ Read the numbers the way the page itself frames them — as British officials' claims in March 1946; the same item credits him with millions more, and the totals in these first reports were inflated (see the affidavit flag on this page: the transport-record figure for Auschwitz is about 1.1 million). Scanned page (Bentley Library): https://digital.bentley.umich.edu/djnews/djn.1946.03.29.001/3
  • The Detroit Jewish News, 12 April 1946, page 48 — JTA, London, under the headline “Oswiecim Camp Commander Sent to Poland for Trial”: “Rudolf Hoess, the former commander of the Oswiecim extermination camp, who is charged with being responsible for the death of 2,000,000 Jews, is to be taken to Poland to stand trial there.” Scanned page: https://digital.bentley.umich.edu/djnews/djn.1946.04.12.001/46
  • Honest gap: the Auschwitz-Birkenau Museum notes his Warsaw trial was announced in newspapers and on radio, and 206 witnesses testified — but this sweep did not verify a specific scanned newspaper article on the March 1947 verdict or the 16 April 1947 hanging, in the American, British, or Jewish press checked. That item stays an open lead rather than a guessed citation.
