O'Donnell's Original Column — "Capitol Stuff," New York Daily News, October 3, 1945

Requested by James on 2026-11-18: the original John O'Donnell column that he later had to apologize for and retract.

Honest status: The New York Daily News is not in Chronicling America, and the full text of the October 3, 1945 column is not freely available online. What survives openly is the column's own words as quoted, word for word, by the other newspapers that answered it. Those exact quoted passages are collected here.

O'Donnell's own words, as quoted by The Detroit Jewish News, October 12, 1945

  • "The General used the words 'yellow bellied' or 'yellow streaked' linking them up with a direct reference to the patient's racial background," O'Donnell wrote.

  • Following the slapping incident, O'Donnell wrote that "Secretary of the Treasury Morgenthau and Associate Justice Frankfurter bellowed in the White House that the important issue was that Patton used the word 'Jew' in reprimanding the reluctant warrior ('after all, what are we fighting this war for anyway, Mr. President?')."

  • O'Donnell wrote further that the removal of General Patton indicates "the secret and astoundingly effective might of this Republic's foreign born political leaders such as Supreme Court Justice Felix Frankfurter, of Vienna, White House advisor David Niles, alias Neyhus, and a Latvian ex-rabbinical student known as Sidney Hillman."

Source for the quotes: The Detroit Jewish News, Oct 12, 1945, page 3 — https://digital.bentley.umich.edu/djnews/djn.1945.10.12.001/3

The fuller version of the Frankfurter passage (found by deep research, 2026-11-18)

Historian Matthew Pressman read the original column itself (New York Daily News, Oct. 3, 1945, p. 4) on Newspapers.com for his article "The New York Daily News and the History of Conservative Media," Modern American History 4 (2021). His rendering of the third passage above is fuller than the Detroit Jewish News version — it restores words Detroit left out:

Patton's removal indicates "the secret and astoundingly effective might of this republic's foreign-born political leaders — such as Justice of the Supreme Court Felix Frankfurter, of Vienna, White House administrative assistant Dave (devious Dave) Niles alias Neyhus and the Latvian ex-rabbinical student now known as Sidney Hillman."

New details vs. the Detroit version: the nickname "(devious Dave)" for David Niles; "White House administrative assistant" (Detroit said "advisor"); "now known as Sidney Hillman." It also confirms the alias is spelled Neyhus — the "Neyhaus" spelling on Wikipedia is a transcription error.

Source: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/modern-american-history/article/new-york-daily-news-and-the-history-of-conservative-media/B3D4FD2CF08C020C4CDAC948786116B2

One caution from the research: The Nation, Oct. 13, 1945, says Patton accompanied the slap with the words "yellow-bellied Jew" — but that may be The Nation's compression of the first passage rather than a literal quote, and the archive OCR is garbled at exactly that word. Do not cite it as verbatim without a page-image check.

Where the complete column can be obtained (deep research verdict, 2026-11-18)

The complete column is not freely accessible anywhere online. It ran on page 4 of the Daily News main edition, Oct. 3, 1945. Doors to the full text:

  • Newspapers.com (paywall) — carries the Daily News backfile; Pressman took the page numbers from the main edition "as archived on newspapers.com."
  • ProQuest Historical Newspapers: U.S. Northeast Collection (library subscription) — includes the NY Daily News 1919–2008/2009; the New York Public Library states it is accessible from home with an NYPL library card.
  • Washington Times-Herald — the column was syndicated there (confirmed by the Detroit Jewish News, Nov. 16, 1945: "Washington Times-Herald, which published John O'Donnell's famous Patton column"); the Times-Herald 1939–1954 is on microfilm at the Library of Congress and DC Public Library.
  • No evidence was found that the Chicago Tribune printed it. The Daily News's own website archive reaches back only to 1996. Not in Chronicling America, not on Fultonhistory, not in Google Books or archive.org full text.
  • Archival lead: a folder "Daily News, New York City [Regarding John O'Donnell Columns], 1945" (Box 57, Folder 14) in the Jewish Federation Council of Greater Los Angeles, Community Relations Committee Collection, Part 2, at CSUN Special Collections, Northridge, CA — contents unverified.

His retraction (October 19, 1945), in his own words

"On Oct. 3 this column attributed the removal of Gen. George S. Patton Jr. from his command of the U. S. 3rd Army... On the evidence, our statements in Capitol Stuff were untrue. We regret having made them."

(as quoted in The Jewish Post, Oct 26, 1945, and The Sentinel, Oct 25, 1945)

Deep search result (2026-11-20 — James asked for a deep search for a free copy)

The full column could NOT be found for free. A deep search on 2026-11-20 checked the Internet Archive (catalog and full-text; no 1945 Daily News or Washington Times-Herald run exists there), Google Books, HathiTrust, Chronicling America (the Daily News is not in it), the free Jewish press (Detroit Jewish News, The Sentinel, Jewish Post, Southern Israelite — all carry excerpts and the refutation only, never the whole column), and the CSUN archival folder "Daily News, New York City [Regarding John O'Donnell Columns], 1945" (Box 57, Folder 14), which is NOT digitized — in-person only at CSUN Special Collections, Northridge, CA.

One door left untested: Fultonhistory.com lists the New York Daily News among its free titles, but the site was unreachable during the search and its year range for the Daily News is unconfirmed — whether it holds October 1945 is unverified. Worth a manual retry when the site is up.

Where the column IS available: - Newspapers.com — Daily News (New York): https://www.newspapers.com/paper/daily-news/7802/ — browse to 1945 → Oct. 3 → page 4 (main edition). Paid: it is a Publisher Extra title; the Daily News-only archive (https://nydailynews.newspapers.com/plans/) runs $7.95/month or $29.95 for 6 months plus tax; Publisher Extra is $74.90 for 6 months. The basic plan will NOT open it. - ProQuest Historical Newspapers (U.S. Northeast Collection): FREE from home with a New York Public Library card — https://libguides.nypl.org/usnewspapers/historicalnews — carries the Daily News 1919–2008/2009 as PDF page images, covering 1945. - Newspapers.com — Washington Times-Herald (which syndicated the column): https://www.newspapers.com/paper/times-herald/37747/ — paid, same plans. - Microfilm, in person, free: Library of Congress (Times-Herald 1939–1954); DC Public Library, Washingtoniana Collection; New York Public Library / New York State Library (Daily News).
