Expanded search — O'Donnell's Oct. 3, 1945 column, sources outside Chronicling America

First expanded-source pass, 2026-11-19. Target: John O'Donnell, "Capitol Stuff," New York Daily News, Oct. 3, 1945 (p. 4) — the column claiming Patton was removed because of Jewish pressure. The Daily News is NOT in Chronicling America. This file records only what this pass found beyond the existing file odonnell-original-column-1945-10-03-quoted-words.md in this folder (Detroit Jewish News Oct. 12, 1945 quotes; Pressman's fuller Frankfurter passage; the doors list; the retraction). It does not repeat that file's contents except to confirm them.

Bottom line first

No new exact wording from the Oct. 3, 1945 column itself was found in any free source checked in this pass. Everything free confirms the passages already saved; the full column remains paywalled (Newspapers.com, ProQuest) or microfilm only (Washington Times-Herald at LOC / DC Public Library). What this pass added is a set of free, non-LOC newspaper sources that document the retraction, the apology, and the protests in the papers' own words.

Pressman article — free full-text PDF, read in full this pass

Matthew Pressman, "The New York Daily News and the History of Conservative Media," Modern American History 4 (2021), 219–238, doi:10.1017/mah.2021.17. Free PDF: https://resolve-he.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/B3D4FD2CF08C020C4CDAC948786116B2/S2515045621000171a.pdf/new_york_daily_news_and_the_history_of_conservative_media.pdf

Read past p. 220 this pass (the O'Donnell passage is on p. 234). Findings:

  • Pressman quotes only the Frankfurter/Niles/Hillman passage already saved in the existing file ("the secret and astoundingly effective might of this republic's foreign-born political leaders—such as Justice of the Supreme Court Felix Frankfurter, of Vienna, White House administrative assistant Dave (devious Dave) Niles alias Neyhus and the Latvian ex-rabbinical student now known as Sidney Hillman"), citing "Capitol Stuff," New York Daily News, Oct. 3, 1945, 4.
  • For the slapping claim, Pressman does not quote the column directly; he paraphrases: O'Donnell claimed the soldier was Jewish and that Patton had made "a direct reference to the patient's racial background" while berating the man. No new column wording there.
  • One new reaction quote (not the column): the Jewish leaders' protest letter published in the Daily News on Oct. 6, 1945 called the column "as flagrantly anti-Semitic as anything which has ever appeared in a metropolitan newspaper."
  • Pressman also confirms the correction/retraction ran in "Capitol Stuff," Oct. 19, 1945, p. 4 (his footnote 103).

Note on a discrepancy: Wikipedia's John O'Donnell article quotes the same Frankfurter passage but spells the alias "Neyhaus" and dates the retraction column "Oct 8, 1945." Pressman (who read the original on Newspapers.com) and the Detroit Jewish News both support Neyhus and the Oct. 19, 1945 apology; Wikipedia's version should not be used.

Free non-LOC newspapers located this pass

  • The Sentinel (Chicago), via Illinois Digital Archives — free PDF downloads. Vol. 140, Issue 4 (Oct. 25, 1945), headline "O'DONNELL RETRACTS FALSEHOOD! / Daily News Offers Apology For Slur On Jews." Its report of the retraction adds a detail: O'Donnell admitted that after "careful investigation we have determined that the one identified soldier whom Patton slapped … is not a Jew, but of German extraction," and that this was revealed the day after the column appeared. PDF: http://www.idaillinois.org/digital/api/collection/p16614coll14/id/54988/download Issue 5 (Nov. 1, 1945) covers the Daily News apology, Rep. Helen Gahagan Douglas, and Mayor La Guardia, and notes O'Donnell "does not apologize for the anti-Semitic implications in his statements concerning [Frankfurter, Niles and Hillman]" and that the retraction "was dictated by Captain Patterson" (per La Guardia). PDF: http://www.idaillinois.org/digital/api/collection/p16614coll14/id/65698/download Caution: the OCR of these PDFs is column-scrambled; exact quotes need a page-image check before being cited as verbatim.
  • The Jewish Post (Indianapolis), via Indiana's free newspaper site and Internet Archive. Oct. 19, 1945: "Daily News Answers Protest, But Does Not Repudiate O'Donnell," quoting the Daily News editorial: "The News is neither antiSemitic nor pro-Semitic; either anti-Catholic nor pro-Catholic…" and "columnists are given wide leeway in expressing their own views." https://newspapers.library.in.gov/?a=is&oid=JPOST19451019-01.1.5&type=staticpdf& Oct. 26, 1945 issue is also on Internet Archive (IA item sim_national-jewish-post-opinion_1945-10-26_13_18): https://ia804505.us.archive.org/32/items/sim_national-jewish-post-opinion_1945-10-26_13_18/sim_national-jewish-post-opinion_1945-10-26_13_18.pdf
  • Detroit Jewish News (free, University of Michigan Bentley archive). Oct. 12, 1945, p. 1 carries the start of the Jewish leaders' protest letter: "In the Daily News of Oct. 3, there appeared a column by John O'Donnell. The content of this column was as flagrantly anti-Semitic…" (continued p. 2) — the same letter Pressman quotes. https://digital.bentley.umich.edu/djnews/djc.1945.10.12.001/1 Nov. 2, 1945, p. 3 prints Patton's denial letter to Joseph Wilner and La Guardia's statement that the retraction was not "'spontaneous' but was dictated by Captain Patterson." https://digital.bentley.umich.edu/djnews/djn.1945.11.02.001/3 Oct. 26, 1945, p. 20 prints the Daily News editorial's own words: "That column was construed as anti-Semitic. Our position is what it always has been. The News is neither anti-Semitic nor pro-Semitic; neither anti-Catholic nor pro-Catholic; neither anti-Protestant nor pro-Protestant…" — editorial/reaction wording, NOT new wording from the Oct. 3 column itself.

JTA archive

Direct retrieval from the JTA archive itself was not achieved in this pass. The wire-level wording is nonetheless reachable free: the Detroit Jewish News and Jewish Post items above are built from JPS/JTA wire copy (bylines visible), so the JTA/JPS reports of the protest and retraction can be read through those free newspaper scans.

HathiTrust / Google Books / Internet Archive — the column itself

No new full text and no new quoted passage from the Oct. 3 column was found in HathiTrust, Google Books, or Internet Archive full text in this pass — consistent with the existing file's verdict. (Internet Archive does hold the Jewish Post issues above, which are reaction coverage, not the column.)

Free vs paywalled — plain statement

  • Free: Pressman's article (PDF above); Detroit Jewish News (Bentley); The Sentinel (Illinois Digital Archives); The Jewish Post (Indiana newspaper site + Internet Archive). All of these give quotes about the column and the already-saved passages — none gives the full column.
  • Paywalled: Newspapers.com (Daily News, Oct. 3, 1945, p. 4) and ProQuest Historical Newspapers (library subscription, e.g. free from home with an NYPL card).
  • Microfilm only: the syndicated run in the Washington Times-Herald, at the Library of Congress and DC Public Library.
  • Unverified lead (from the existing file): CSUN Special Collections, Jewish Federation Council of Greater Los Angeles, Community Relations Committee Collection Part 2, Box 57 Folder 14, "Daily News, New York City [Regarding John O'Donnell Columns], 1945" — contents still unverified.

"Not found" above means not verified through the free routes checked in this pass — not that the text does not exist. The full column exists; it is simply behind a paywall or on microfilm.
