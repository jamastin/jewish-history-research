General Patton — Article 2: The Waterbury Democrat

  • Newspaper: The Waterbury Democrat — Waterbury, Conn.
  • Date: October 2, 1945 — Image 1 (page 1)
  • Headline: "Liberated Jews Deny Charges Of Neglect" (same page: "Patton Loses Command Of U. S. Third Army")
  • Link: https://www.loc.gov/resource/sn82014085/1945-10-02/ed-1/?sp=1&q=Patton+Jews
  • Source: Library of Congress, Chronicling America

Brief statement, in the paper's own words

"Until Eisenhower's visit, it was apparent that Patton took little personal interest in Jewish welfare here."

From the same article (United Press, Feldafing, Bavaria): "Gen. Dwight D. Eisenhower himself visited this and other Bavarian camps two weeks ago and immediately ordered the military governor of Bavaria, Gen. George S. Patton to seize neighboring German dwellings to relieve congested conditions for the Jews."
