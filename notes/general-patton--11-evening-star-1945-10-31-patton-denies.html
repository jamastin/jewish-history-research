General Patton — Article 11: The Evening Star

  • Newspaper: The Evening Star — Washington, D.C.
  • Date: October 31, 1945 — Image 27 (page 27)
  • Headline: "Patton Denies Making Remarks Against Jews"
  • Link: https://www.loc.gov/resource/sn83045462/1945-10-31/ed-1/?sp=27&q=Patton+Jewish
  • Source: Library of Congress, Chronicling America

Brief statement, in the paper's own words

Patton's own denial, by cable from Germany: "I am glad to have the opportunity of categorically denying that I have ever made any statement contrary to the Jewish or any other religious faith."
