FOUND — the "slaves to gold" passage (album item M062)

Verified in Chronicling America on 2026-11-19. James was right: it IS a newspaper article on the Chronicling America / loc.gov site.

The source

  • Article: "THE NEW DEPARTURE"
  • Author: Stephen D. Dillaye
  • Newspaper: The Anti-Monopolist — Saint Paul, Minnesota
  • Date: April 4, 1878
  • Page: 4 (Image 4 of 8), LCCN sn85025587
  • Page link: https://www.loc.gov/resource/sn85025587/1878-04-04/ed-1/?sp=4

The passage, exactly as printed (verified in the page's text)

"The Greenback is the new departure. It is America for America. It is the American financial policy. It is the people for the people. It is the nation saying to all its enemies, 'hands off, this is our money. Gold is good, but it is scarce, foreigners control it. Greenbacks are better. They are from the people for the people. They are the nation for the nation. They are Americans.' To be free, a nation must control its own money. To be slaves to gold is to be slaves to the Rothchilds and German Jews. With Greenbacks for our currency and a money policy of our own free from all banking interference, (and above and beyond all foreign interference) which shall furnish an adequate supply for a perfect equality of right, such will give labor its reward and industry its recompense—this is the American policy."

Also on the same page

  • A piece headed "THE GREENBACK WAS BORN" (the archive's text scan misreads it as "GREENRACK"), including: "He has killed gold, which the red-fisted villains the Rothchilds employed to murder him."

How it was found (2026-11-19)

Direct search inside loc.gov's Chronicling America page search, 1868–1900. "Slaves to gold" alone gave 48 results; the phrase "Rothchilds and German Jews" pinned this page. The wording was verified directly in the page text — every fragment from James's album page (M062) appears in this one continuous passage.

What this closes

Ruled out before the find, and now settled as NOT the source: Coughlin's Social Justice Feb. 12 & 19, 1940; the 2014 Sacred Truth pamphlet reprint; The New Ensign 2022 reprint; Peter Cooper (1880); and ten Greenback/Populist-era books read in full (Emery, Heath ×2, Usher, Weaver, Harvey, Berkey, Brice, Walbert, Donnelly). The passage is Greenback Party era, 1878, exactly as its type and language suggested.
