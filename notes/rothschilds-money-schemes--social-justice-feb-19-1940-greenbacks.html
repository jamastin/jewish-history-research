Social Justice, Feb. 19, 1940 — the greenback article (Part Two)

Checked live on archive.org, 2026-11-19 (Nov 18 evening local).

What this is

Coughlin's "Abraham Lincoln and Rothschilds" ran in two parts in Social Justice:

  • Part One — Feb. 12, 1940 (Vol. 5A, No. 7), pp. 8–9, continued p. 18: the plot to divide the Union, the 1857 London meeting, Judah Benjamin as Rothschild agent. Ends: "(A second article will tell how Lincoln wrestled with the international financiers in order to give America an honest money system — how he won — and the real reason why he was assassinated.)"
  • Full text: https://archive.org/download/socnew_1940_vol05a_07/socnew_1940_vol05a_07_text.pdf
  • Part Two — Feb. 19, 1940 (Vol. 5A, No. 8), "Part Two — U.S. Issue of Debt-Free Money", pp. 10–13: this is the greenback article.
  • Full text: https://archive.org/download/socnew_1940_vol05a_07/socnew_1940_vol05a_08_text.pdf

Correction to an earlier note in this folder: the Feb. 19 issue was previously recorded as "checked and ruled out." It was not ruled out for the greenback search — Part Two is the greenback piece, with the Hazard Circular quoted in full.

Exact words from Part Two (Feb. 19, 1940)

The Hazard Circular (Bank of England, July 1862, as quoted here — the chattel-slavery-to-debt-slavery passage James asked about):

"Slavery is likely to be abolished by the war power and chattel slavery destroyed. This, I and my European friends are glad of, for slavery is but the owning of labor and carries with it the care of the laborers, while the European plan, led by England, is that capital shall control labor by controlling wages."

On Lincoln and financial slavery:

"He was determined to have a Constitutional money system because he saw that financial slavery was only less subtle than chattel slavery, a little more refined, but in the end, [frightful]..."

The Rothschild letter of June 25, 1863 (Rothschild Brothers to Ikleheimer, Morton and Vandergould, No. 3 Wall Street, New York), as quoted here:

"[If the United States Government issuing interest-free and debt-free money] ... That government must be destroyed or it will destroy every Monarchy on the globe!"

Greenbacks vs. gold (same article): the bankers bought greenbacks on Wall Street for 35 cents on the dollar; gold rose to ~$2.85 measured in greenbacks; the $450-million of greenbacks were converted into $1-billion of bonded indebtedness.

What is still NOT verified

The exact album sentence — "To be free, a nation must control its own money. To be slaves to gold is to be slaves to the Rothchilds and German Jews. With Greenbacks for our currency…" — does not appear in the archive.org OCR text of either the Feb. 12 or the Feb. 19, 1940 issue (searched for "slaves to gold", "Greenback(s)", "control its own money", "German Jews" — no hits). Possibilities: the OCR garbled it, the album page comes from the later pamphlet reprint (Abraham Lincoln and the Rothschilds, reprinting the Feb. 12 and 19 articles, which may carry extra wording), or it comes from a different source quoting Coughlin. The Feb. 19 article is the right piece and the right subject — but the exact sentence itself is still unverified as a Social Justice quote. Next check if James wants it: the pamphlet reprint text (e.g. unz.com / archive.org copies of Abraham Lincoln and the Rothschilds).
