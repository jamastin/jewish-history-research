Expanded search — the greenback pamphlet "Abraham Lincoln and the Rothschilds"

First expanded-source pass, 2026-11-19. Target: the sentence in the album (3 copies) — "To be free, a nation must control its own money. To be slaves to gold is to be slaves to the Rothchilds and German Jews. With Greenbacks for our currency…" — which does NOT appear in the archive.org OCR of Social Justice, Feb. 12, 1940 (Part One) or Feb. 19, 1940 (Part Two). Standing lead: the pamphlet reprint of those two articles.

What the pamphlet actually is

It is a modern reprint booklet, not a 1940 pamphlet:

  • WorldCat record (OCLC 1287936942): Abraham Lincoln and the Rothschilds: the real cause of the Civil War — Charles E. Coughlin (author), Robert Alan Balaicius (author), Sacred Truth Publishing (issuing body). Print book, English, 2014, Sacred Truth Publishing, Mountain City, Tennessee. https://search.worldcat.org/title/1287936942 (WorldCat would not show the list of holding libraries without a location — "Location not available" — so which libraries hold it is still unknown.)
  • Publisher's own catalog (Sacred Truth Publishing, updated Aug. 2022) describes the contents exactly: the Coughlin part is 13 pages, "re-type-set with illustrations added," "[originally published in the Feb. 12th and Feb. 19th 1940 issues of Rev. Charles E. Coughlin's newsletter Social Justice]"; added to it is a 27-page appendix by R.A.B. (Robert Alan Balaicius): "an In-Depth Appendix concerning The Jewish ancestry of John Wilkes Booth and Various Questions Concerning the Ancestry and Religious Faith of Abraham Lincoln." 40 pages total, priced $4.00–$4.50 + postage. https://sacredtruthministries.com/system/files/resources/Book-list-8-5x11-Race-Civilization-Exposing%20Political%20Corruption-Conspiracies-Aug-2022.pdf
  • The same booklet is still listed by Watchman Outreach Ministries (literature catalog, June 2026) at $4.00 as "Abraham Lincoln and The Rothschilds by Coughlin: Instigators of the American Civil War." Resale asking prices seen on eBay/PicClick summaries: $30.00 and $42.75 "Buy It Now" (asking prices only, not sold prices).

Scanned copy — Internet Archive (images only, no text layer)

  • https://archive.org/details/img-9541_202606 — "Abraham Lincoln and The Rothschilds: Rev. Charles E. Coughlin (and Robert Alan Balaicius for the added sections)," uploaded by ForgottenVolumes. A second reprint, fully text-searchable — The New Ensign (2022)

The New Ensign magazine reprinted the same Coughlin article in two parts, and both parts are on Internet Archive as searchable full text:

  • No. 152, April 2022, pp. 31–33 — Part One (the Feb. 12, 1940 article). Full text: http://ia801603.us.archive.org/6/items/new_ensign_152/new_ensign_152_djvu.txt Searches run on the full text: "slaves to gold" — no match; "German Jews" — no match; "Greenback" — no match; "control its own money" — no match. The reprint there ends "To be Continued," i.e. it is only Part One. Exact lines it does contain: the article opens "TEXTBOOKS that have been carefully edited continue to teach American school children that the American Civil War was fought over the Slavery issue…" and says Lincoln was assassinated "because he was determined that the United States be free from the bondage of the international bankers."
  • No. 153, May 2022, pp. 27–29 — Part Two (the Feb. 19, 1940 greenback article). Full text: https://archive.org/download/new_ensign_153/new_ensign_153_djvu.txt Searches run on the full text: "slaves to gold" — no match; "German Jews" — no match; "control its own money" — no match. "Greenback" appears 13 times, and the text matches the Feb. 19, 1940 Social Justice article already saved in this folder, including the Hazard Circular ("…it will not do to allow the greenback, as it is called, to circulate as money any length of time, as we cannot control that") and "financial slavery was only less subtle than chattel slavery."

So both parts of Coughlin's article, in a second independent reprint, still do not contain the album sentence.

Other catalogs checked

  • Library of Congress catalog (catalog.loc.gov): a web search aimed at the LOC catalog produced no LOC catalog record for the pamphlet — results were bookseller, WorldCat, Facebook and Internet Archive items only. That is "not found this way," not proof LOC holds no copy.
  • HathiTrust: searches produced no pamphlet record (results were unrelated Social Justice material).
  • Google Books: no dedicated record for the pamphlet was retrieved in the searches run.
  • General web search for the exact sentence ("slaves to gold" + Rothschilds + "German Jews" + greenback): no occurrence found anywhere on the open web. Top hits were unrelated books (Stephen Goodson's A History of Central Banking and the Enslavement of Mankind; Gerald B. Winrod's The Defender, June 1934 — which contains Rothschild/greenback-era material and the June 25, 1863 Rothschild Brothers letter, but not the target sentence in the text returned).

Honest status of Target 1

  • The pamphlet is pinned down: 2014 Sacred Truth Publishing reprint of the two Feb. 1940 articles (13 pp.) plus a 27-page appendix by Robert Alan Balaicius that is NOT part of the 1940 articles; WorldCat OCLC 1287936942; a free scan exists on Internet Archive as page images only.
  • The album sentence is still NOT found in any searchable text checked: not in Social Justice Feb. 12 / Feb. 19, 1940 OCR, not in either part of the New Ensign 2022 reprint, not in general web search.
  • It could still be in: (a) the Balaicius appendix (pp. 14–40 of the booklet — modern added text, not Coughlin's 1940 words), (b) the album page's own added caption/headline rather than the article text, or (c) OCR-garbled in a source not yet checked.
  • Next step: read the 22 page images of the Internet Archive scan (img-9541_202606) one by one — that is the only remaining free copy of the actual Sacred Truth booklet, appendix included.

Page-by-page read of the Internet Archive scan — 2026-11-19

The 22 photographs of item img-9541_202606 were downloaded in full and read. Method: each photo was EXIF-rotated, OCR'd locally with Tesseract 5.3.4 (500–770 words recovered per spread — clean, readable type), the full OCR text was searched for every target phrase and its parts, and the key spreads were also read visually off the photographs to catch anything OCR might have garbled (cover; pp. ii+3; pp. 8–9; pp. 10–11; pp. 12–13; pp. 14–15; pp. 28–29; p. 40).

Coverage — the scan is the COMPLETE booklet, appendix included

Each photograph after the cover is a two-page spread. Page numbers printed in the booklet itself pin the sequence down exactly:

  • IMG_9530 = front cover; IMG_9532 = title page; IMG_9533 = pp. ii + 3
  • IMG_9534–IMG_9539 = pp. 4–15 (IMG_9537 is pp. 10–11; IMG_9539 is pp. 14–15)
  • IMG_9540–IMG_9551 = pp. 16–39 (IMG_9541 contains p. 18; IMG_9545 p. 26; IMG_9551 p. 38)
  • IMG_9552 = p. 40, the last page

So all 40 pages are photographed — nothing is missing. The Coughlin 1940 reprint runs pp. 3–15 (Part One opens p. 3; Part Two opens p. 9), and the Balaicius appendix begins partway down p. 15 under the printed heading "—— Appendix by Modern Publisher ——" and runs to p. 40. The appendix IS included in this scan, in full.

Result — NO match. The album sentence is not in this booklet.

Searched across all 22 OCR'd pages, in both the Coughlin part and the appendix: "slaves to gold" — no match; "Rothchilds" (the album's spelling) — no match (the booklet always spells "Rothschilds"); "German Jews" — no match; "control its own money" — no match; "To be free, a nation must…" — no match. "Greenback(s)" appears many times, but never in the album sentence or anything like it.

Closest passages in the booklet (exact words, for the record):

  • p. ii (publisher's front matter): "Let us issue and control the money of a nation and we care not who make its laws." — attributed there to "Amschel Mayer Bauer (1743-1812)."
  • pp. 9–10, the Hazard Circular as reprinted: "…while the European plan, led by England, is that capital shall control labor by controlling wages. This can be done by controlling the money."
  • p. 11: Lincoln "saw that financial slavery was only less subtle than chattel slavery, a little more refined, but in the end, the cruel, despotic exploitation of the American people."
  • p. 14, the quoted 1865 London Times editorial: "That government must be destroyed or it will destroy every Monarchy on the globe!"
  • p. 28 (appendix): Lincoln "was murdered partly because he was wanting to free the slaves—but send them all back to Africa; and also because he was in the process… of paying off the National Debt without borrowing money from the Jews, by his system of Greenbacks."

Updated status of Target 1

The pamphlet door is now CLOSED, not left open: the only free copy of the 2014 Sacred Truth booklet has been read cover to cover, and the album sentence is in neither Coughlin's reprinted 1940 text nor Balaicius's 27-page appendix. Combined with the earlier checks (Social Justice Feb. 12 / Feb. 19, 1940 OCR; The New Ensign 2022 reprint; general web search), the sentence "To be slaves to gold is to be slaves to the Rothchilds and German Jews" has not been found in any version of the Coughlin article or its reprints. Remaining possibilities are the ones already noted: the album page's own added caption/headline (i.e. the sentence may be a later summarizer's words printed on the album page itself, not a quote from 1940), or a different source altogether that quotes or paraphrases Coughlin's greenback argument.
