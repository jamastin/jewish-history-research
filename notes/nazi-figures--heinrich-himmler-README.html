Heinrich Himmler (1900–1945)

Heinrich Himmler (Bundesarchiv Bild 183-S72707).

Who he was: Reichsführer-SS from 1929 and, from 1936, Chief of the German Police. He built the SS into the organisation that ran the concentration camps and, through the Reich Security Main Office and the SS economic offices under him, carried out the murder of Europe's Jews. He killed himself in British custody on 23 May 1945, so he was never tried.

Key documents

1. The Posen speeches — 4 and 6 October 1943

Speaking to SS-Gruppenführer at Posen (Poznań) on 4 October 1943, Himmler dropped the regime's euphemisms in a closed room and spoke of “the evacuation of the Jews, the extermination of the Jewish people” as a task being carried out — and as something never to be spoken of publicly. A second speech followed on 6 October to Reichsleiter and Gauleiter. The 4 October speech survives in text (Nuremberg document 1919-PS) and in audio recordings. - Read the excerpt (GHDI, from the Nuremberg record): https://germanhistorydocs.ghi-dc.org/print_document.cfm?document_id=1513 - Hear excerpts in his own voice, with the transcript: https://www.historyplace.com/worldwar2/holocaust/h-posen.htm

2. His service calendars (Dienstkalender)

Himmler's appointment calendars record his days meeting by meeting — including his conferences with Hitler. The 1941/42 calendar was published in a scholarly edition: Der Dienstkalender Heinrich Himmlers 1941/42, edited by Peter Witte and others (Hamburg, 1999). Further calendars (1940; 1943–1945, found in a Moscow archive) have since been published. They are a working record, not a diary of opinions — their value is in dates, attendees and subjects. - Catalogue record for the 1941/42 edition: https://openlibrary.org/works/OL1455680W/Der_Dienstkalender_Heinrich_Himmlers_1941_42?edition=key%3A%2Fbooks%2FOL6839026M&mode=all

3. The Korherr correspondence — April 1943

SS statistician Richard Korherr's report to Himmler (23 March 1943) counted the “Final Solution” in numbers. Himmler's office then ordered the wording softened: his adjutant Rudolf Brandt wrote to Korherr on 10 April 1943 that the Reichsführer-SS wished the term “special treatment of the Jews” (Sonderbehandlung) to appear nowhere, and that page 9 was to speak instead of Jews being “sifted through the camps.” Himmler had called the report well suited “for purposes of camouflage.” - The Korherr Report itself (GHDI): https://germanhistorydocs.org/en/nazi-germany-1933-1945/statistical-report-on-the-final-solution-known-as-the-korherr-report-march-23-1943

4. Memorandum on the peoples of the East — 25 May 1940

In his secret memorandum to Hitler, Reflections on the Treatment of Peoples of Alien Races in the East (Nuremberg document NO-1880), Himmler proposed breaking the peoples of the East into splinters, limiting non-German schooling to signing one's name and counting to 500, taking children judged “of good blood” to Germany, and — for the Jews — wrote that he hoped “the concept of Jews will be completely extinguished through the possibility of large-scale emigration of all Jews to Africa or some other colony.” - Read the memorandum: https://jewishvirtuallibrary.org/jsource/Holocaust/himmler3.html

Researched 2026-11-21. The Posen audio link is a long-standing educational site; the recordings themselves are archival (NARA/DRA holdings).

In the papers

Newspaper coverage of Himmler, verified 2026-11-21.

  • Herald and News (Klamath Falls, Oregon), 24 May 1945, page 1 — the day after his suicide: “Heinrich Himmler bit into [a] vial of poison and killed himself last night three days after his capture by the British second army.” The same report notes he had shaved off his moustache and wore a black patch over one eye, and that “Himmler, who vied with Hitler in blackening the pages of German history, robbed the allies of their No. 1 war criminal.” Scanned page (University of Oregon newspaper archive): https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn99063813/1945-05-24/ed-1/seq-1.pdf
  • Detroit Jewish Chronicle, 1 June 1945, page 4 — the Jewish press's verdict, in a letter/comment column: “Heinrich Himmler, head of the Gestapo, who committed suicide by swallowing some poison when the British captured him, will go down in history as the greatest murderer who ever lived.” Scanned page (Detroit Jewish News digital archive, Bentley Library): https://digital.bentley.umich.edu/djnews/djc.1945.06.01.001/4
  • The Detroit Jewish News, 29 March 1946, page 3 — Himmler named from the witness stand at Nuremberg: “Ex-Reichsmarshal Herman Goering, testifying in his defense at the Nuremberg War Crime Trial, told the International Military Tribunal that both Joseph Goebbels and Heinrich Himmler were more rabid Jew-baiters than he.” Scanned page: https://digital.bentley.umich.edu/djnews/djn.1946.03.29.001/3
  • Honest gap: the Posen speech entered the Nuremberg record as document 1919-PS, but this sweep did not verify a specific newspaper article from the time about the speech being presented there — so none is listed, rather than padding the page.
