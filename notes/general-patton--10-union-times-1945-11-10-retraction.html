General Patton — Article 10: The Union Times

  • Newspaper: The Union Times — New Haven, Conn.
  • Date: November 10, 1945 — Image 4 (page 4)
  • Headline: "Retraction" (column) — "New York Daily News Commentator Retracts Story On Gen. Patton, Jews"
  • Link: https://www.loc.gov/resource/sn92060454/1945-11-10/ed-1/?sp=4&q=Patton+Jews
  • Source: Library of Congress, Chronicling America

Brief statement, in the paper's own words

"Sixteen days after it was proven absolutely false, New York Daily News columnist John O'Donnell retracted his allegation, made in his October 3 column, that pressure from influential American Jews caused the removal of General George S. Patton from command of the U.S. Third Army."
