Rothschilds Money Schemes — new research topic (opened 2026-11-18)

Subfolder of "where did we go wrong," titled by James: Rothschilds Money Schemes.

What James asked to search (2026-11-18)

  • The greenbacks and the Rothschilds, in the era of the demonetization of silver.
  • Anything Rothschild — or bankers generally — said or were reported saying about the greenback.
  • Rothschilds buying up silver from India.
  • Rothschilds dumping silver in the United States.
  • Rothschild control of gold, in France and in Britain.
  • The article James remembers reading with the idea: to be slaves to gold is to be slaves to the Rothschilds and the German Jews (exact wording not yet found — a standing target for this search).
  • James said: "Use your imagination to pull up articles about that" — i.e., try varied phrasings and related angles in the old newspapers, exact words as always.
  • Added by James (2026-11-18): also search for anything where Rothschild or the bankers/money-power writers mention chattel slavery moving to debt slavery — the shift from owning people outright to holding them through debt.

First finds (2026-11-18, first pass — not yet saved as individual articles)

  1. New York Times, March 8, 1931 (looking back at the early 1890s): "THE ROTHSCHILDS WERE ACCUSED OF HAVING CONSPIRED TO DEMONETIZE SILVER AND CORNER THE GOLD SUPPLY." (Quoted in Charles Savoie's compilation of period NYT silver coverage: http://nosilvernationalization.org/75.pdf)

  2. Senate Subcommittee on Silver, quoted in the NYT, February 12, 1931: "THE BRITISH GOVERNMENT FOR INDIA, SINCE 1928, HAS DUMPED LARGE QUANTITIES OF SILVER UPON THE MARKETS OF THE WORLD, AND IS CONTINUING SUCH POLICY AND PRACTICE, NOTWITHSTANDING THE ALARMINGLY LOW PRICE OF SILVER. ACCORDING TO CREDITABLE REPORTS, THE GOVERNMENT FOR INDIA STILL HAS AVAILABLE 400,000,000 OUNCES OF SILVER THAT IT CAN AND WILL DUMP UPON THE MARKETS OF THE WORLD."

  3. Senator Key Pittman, April 1931 (quoted in Herbert M. Bratter, "The Silver Episode," Journal of Political Economy, Oct. 1938): "We cannot get a conference, so we have quit trying to do that. All we can do is to remove or offset the unnatural dumping of silver by India."

  4. Bratter (1938), the documented scale: "From 1927 through 1932 the Indian government sold an estimated 155,200,000 fine ounces of silver... Sales of demonetized silver by all countries from 1920 through 1932 totaled about 541,000,000 ounces." And: "since 1926 the Indian authorities had been the principal sellers of demonetized silver." (Full text: https://archive.org/stream/sim_journal-of-political-economy_1938-10_46_5/sim_journal-of-political-economy_1938-10_46_5_djvu.txt)

  5. McClure's Magazine, August 1911 (on Rothschild gold control): "...the Rothschild control of international money markets through its manipulation of the price of gold. Each day, the world price of gold is set in the London office of N.M. Rothschild and Company." (As excerpted in Eustace Mullins, The Secrets of the Federal Reserve.)

  6. The Pike County Democrat (Petersburg, Indiana), April 22, 1898 — Hon. J. G. Shanklin's Indianapolis speech (April 7, 1898) on bimetallism: silver "was demonetized by dropping the silver dollar out of the list of legal coins... the fact of the demonetization of silver was [not] realized, for it was accomplished stealthily"; on the "greed of gold syndicates" and bonds "redeemable in gold, thus doubling their value to the holders." A real period newspaper item in the Indiana newspaper archive (Chronicling America partner): https://newspapers.library.in.gov/?a=is&oid=PICD18980422.1.1&type=staticpdf&
