General Patton — Article 1: The Commentator

  • Newspaper: Commentator of the Community Council — Jacksonville, Fla.
  • Date: October 11, 1945 — Image 4 (page 4)
  • Headline: "Gen. Patton Ordered Return of Jews To Poland, Where They Face Death"
  • Link: https://www.loc.gov/resource/sn95026724/1945-10-11/ed-1/?sp=4&q=Patton+Jews
  • Source: Library of Congress, Chronicling America

Brief statement, in the paper's own words

"The forced return to Poland of 600 Jews who had fled into American-held territory in Czechoslovakia to escape anti-Semitic terror, was ordered by General George S. Patton's headquarters."

Also in the article: "Jews alone were singled out for forcible return."
