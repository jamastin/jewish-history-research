"Roosevelt Was Jewish" — The Claims About Both Presidents

Researched 2026-11-22 for the names-and-identity topic. Built from the saved items (album item M152 and the "Roosevelt, rosenfeld" Chrome bookmarks) plus an extensive web search.

How this file is organised: period claims are quoted verbatim and labelled CLAIM. What the documented record shows is labelled RECORD. Anything that could not be checked is labelled UNVERIFIED or QUEUED. Nothing here is dressed up: where a site blocked us, it says so.

PART 1 — THEODORE ROOSEVELT (president 1901–1909)

1A. The "police officer / sheriff in a Jewish neighbourhood" memory — MATCHED, twice over

Two separate real things sit behind the claim:

(1) The job itself (RECORD). Theodore Roosevelt was New York City Police Commissioner from spring 1895 to 1897 — in his own words, from Theodore Roosevelt: An Autobiography (1913), Chapter VI, "The New York Police":

"In the spring of 1895 I was appointed by Mayor Strong Police Commissioner, and I served as President of the Police Commission of New York for the two following years."

He was not a sheriff and not a beat policeman — he ran the whole department — but "police commissioner of New York" is the documented fact the memory points at. (Source: https://standardebooks.org/ebooks/theodore-roosevelt/an-autobiography/text/chapter-6)

(2) The Jewish episode from his police years (RECORD, in his own words). While commissioner, the German antisemitic preacher Rector Hermann Ahlwardt came to New York. New York's Jews asked Roosevelt to stop him speaking. From the same chapter of the Autobiography:

"While I was Police Commissioner an antisemitic preacher from Berlin, Rector Ahlwardt, came over to New York to preach a crusade against the Jews. Many of the New York Jews were much excited and asked me to prevent him from speaking and not to give him police protection. This, I told them, was impossible; and if possible would have been undesirable because it would have made him a martyr. The proper thing to do was to make him ridiculous. Accordingly I detailed for his protection a Jew sergeant and a score or two of Jew policemen. He made his harangue against the Jews under the active protection of some forty policemen, every one of them a Jew! It was the most effective possible answer; and incidentally it was an object-lesson to our people, whose greatest need it is to learn that there must be no division by class hatred, whether this hatred be that of creed against creed, nationality against nationality, section against section…"

A modern retelling (Jewish Book Council, "Combating Antisemitism: Lessons from Theodore Roosevelt") adds that the lecture was at Cooper Union, in the Jewish Lower East Side, and quotes Roosevelt instructing a deputy to pick officers with "the most pronounced Hebrew physiognomy" — that extra wording is from the retelling, not from the Autobiography text checked tonight, so treat the added quotes as UNVERIFIED. (Source: https://www.jewishbookcouncil.org/pb-daily/combating-antisemitism-lessons-from-theodore-roosevelt)

(3) The same claim may also come straight off the M152 page — see Part 3. The 1914 newspaper feature behind M152 says, in one breath, that Roosevelt was of Jewish descent AND that "Mr. Roosevelt was a policeman for many years before he entered political life." One page explains both halves of the claim.

1B. The Spanish-American War (1898) — Jews under Roosevelt, and the "revenge" theme

(RECORD) Jewish soldiers served under Roosevelt in Cuba, and Jewish veterans made him an honorary member. From the official timeline of the Jewish War Veterans of the USA (jwv.org), entry for 1900:

"Hebrew Veterans of the War with Spain — Jews who served in the war with Spain organized the Hebrew Veterans of the War with Spain. President Theodore Roosevelt, who had a Jewish contingent among his Rough Riders in the War with Spain, was named an honorary member."

(Source: https://www.jwv.org/wp-content/uploads/JWV-History-Timeline-Final-2.pdf)

About five thousand Jews served in the war overall (figure given in the Sotheby's catalogue note for Rabbi Joshua Seigel's 1898 prayer for American victory, citing Jewish Theological Seminary material; the same note says "much of the Jewish press in Hebrew, Yiddish, Judeo-Spanish, and, of course, English closely followed the developments"). Cyrus Adler published a "Preliminary List of Jewish Soldiers and Sailors Who Served in the Spanish-American War" in The American Jewish Year Book 2 (1900–1901), pp. 525–622. (Source: https://www.sothebys.com/en/buy/auction/2022/important-judaica/prayer-for-american-victory-in-the-spanish)

(CLAIM/THEME — documented) "Revenge on Spain" was a real theme of the era. Jewish support for Cuba's fight against Spain was openly framed as payback for 1492 and the Inquisition. The clearest verbatim example found: Louis Fine, the Vilnius-born leader of Jewish religious life in Key West, described his work sending men and ammunition to the Cuban rebels, in the biography he approved for Prominent Jews of America (1918), as:

"a part of Jewish vengeance against Spain, the old persecutor of the race."

Key West's Jewish Cuban-independence cell, founded December 1892 with José Martí's encouragement, named itself Club Abrabanel — after Don Isaac Abrabanel, the Jewish statesman who defied Spain at the expulsion. (Source, a modern article quoting the 1918 book: https://www.theoccidentalobserver.net/2026/09/30/the-forgotten-jewish-network-behind-cubas-fight-against-spain/ — the Fine quote is presented there as Fine's own approved wording; the 1918 book itself was not opened tonight, so the quote is verified only through that article.)

(NOT FOUND tonight — QUEUED) The specific item — a Yiddish or Jewish newspaper of the war era saying Roosevelt himself "got revenge for them" — could not be verified tonight. Both archives that hold 1898 Yiddish/Jewish papers blocked us (Chronicling America 403; JPress 403). Nothing was invented to fill the gap. Exact searches queued for when the sites open are listed at the end of this file. One caution for the hunt: in 1898 Roosevelt was not yet president — he was Assistant Secretary of the Navy, then lieutenant colonel/colonel of the Rough Riders — so the paper would name him as Colonel Roosevelt, or the item may be a later (1901+) retelling of the war.

1C. Was Theodore Roosevelt himself claimed to be Jewish?

Yes — the same family claim covered both presidents (see Part 2). The piece aimed most directly at TR's generation is in the Chase Osborn letters (Part 2B): Osborn wrote to editor Philip Slomovitz on March 21, 1935 (as printed in Slomovitz's book Purely Commentary, 1981, and quoted in later write-ups):

"President Roosevelt knows well enough that his ancestors were Jewish. I heard Theodore Roosevelt state twice that his ancestors were Jewish. Once was to me when I asked him about it after he had made a pleasing euphemistic statement in a speech to a Jewish gathering"

That is Osborn's CLAIM, printed 36 years after the alleged conversations. No independent record of TR saying this was found. (RECORD note: historian Frank Freidel, "The Dutchness of the Roosevelts," documents that both Roosevelts played up whatever ancestry suited the audience they were addressing — TR "could express his pride over not only his Holland but also his French Huguenot and English" forebears and more. Source: https://bmgn-lchr.nl/article/download/URN:NBN:NL:UI:10-1-102568/799/876)

PART 2 — FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT (president 1933–1945)

2A. The "Rosenfeld" name claim — where it actually comes from

Nobody in the period record produced a document showing a "Rosenfeld." What the record shows is a chain of claims:

Step 1 — the Rossacampo story (CLAIM, 1934). Chase S. Osborn, former governor of Michigan and a friend of both Roosevelts, gave an interview carried by the St. Petersburg (Fla.) Times on February 8, 1934, saying the Roosevelts descend from a Spanish Jewish family. As the story was reprinted in Gerald B. Winrod's The Defender, September 1937, quoting the French paper Voix d'Alsace et de Lorraine:

"Roosevelt is of Jewish blood. An ancestor, Rossacampo, driven out of Spain in 1620, went to Holland where the name was variously changed from Rossacampo to Rosenthal, Rosenblum, Rosenbaum, Rosenburg and Rosenvelt. "After coming to America in 1649 Claes Rosevelt changed his first name to Nicolaes, entered the clothing business and married in New York in 1687. One of his sons, still a zealous Israelite, married Catherine Hardenburg, and from this branch President Roosevelt and his wife, who is his first cousin, descended."

(Source — full text of The Defender, Sept. 1937: https://archive.org/stream/sim_defender_1937-09_12_5/sim_defender_1937-09_12_5_djvu.txt)

The Osborn interview text, as quoted from the Florida paper in Slomovitz's book and later reproductions:

"He (Osborn) referred to the 'Jewish ancestry' of the president, explaining how he is a descendant of the Rossacampo family expelled from Spain in 1620. Seeking safety in Germany, Holland and other countries, members of the family, he said, changed their name to Rosenberg, Rosenbaum, Rosenblum, Rosenvelt and Rosenthal. The Rosenvelts in north Holland finally became Roosevelt, soon becoming apostates with the first generation and others following suit until, in the fourth generation a little storekeeper by the name of Jacobus Roosevelt was the only one who remained true to his Jewish faith. It is because of this Jewish ancestry, Osborn said, that President Roosevelt has the trend of economic safety in his hands."

Step 2 — the genealogy chart (CLAIM by spin, 1934–1936). A genealogical chart of the Roosevelt family prepared at the Carnegie Institution of Washington under Dr. H. H. Laughlin (carried by an Associated Press dispatch of March 7, 1934, and printed in the Washington Star, February 29, 1934) was then circulated by Winrod's The Revelator (Wichita, Kansas), October 18, 1936, under the headline "Roosevelt's Jewish Ancestry," claiming:

"Even a hasty perusal of this factual document convinces one as to the President's Jewish ancestry… It shows why he has given hundreds of so-called Liberals, Socialists and Communists powerful positions in the national government."

The chart itself, as reproduced there, lists ordinary Dutch names — Claes Martenszen van Rosenvelt, Jannetje Samuels, Heyltje Jans, Catharina Hardenbroek, and so on. The "Jewish" reading was the pamphleteers', not the chart's. (Source: https://israelect.com/reference/WillieMartin/ROOSEV~1%20%5bD%5d.htm — a far-right archive site hosting the reprint; used here only as the surviving copy of the 1936 period text.)

Step 3 — "Rosenfeld" as a Nazi-era slur-name (RECORD of usage). German and American Nazi propagandists took to calling the president "Rosenfeld." Randall Bytwerk's Julius Streicher (Cooper Square Press, 2001) notes of Fritz Julius Kuhn's German-American Bund rally rhetoric that Kuhn referred to "President Franklin D. Rosenfeld using a typical Jewish name. Many Nazis believed that the President was a Jew who had changed his name." Japanese wartime writers repeated the same rumour ("according to rumor, his real name had been Rosenfeld" — Shofar 11:3, Spring 1993, on Jews under Japanese occupation). So "Rosenfeld" was a name pinned ON Roosevelt by enemies — no period source shows Roosevelt or his family ever using it.

2B. FDR's own answer — the Slomovitz letters, 1935 (RECORD)

Philip Slomovitz, editor of the Detroit Jewish Chronicle, saw the Osborn story repeated in a Michigan publication (the Civic Echo) and wrote directly to the president on March 4, 1935, asking whether his family records lent "affirmation or denial to these fantastic stories." FDR replied on March 7, 1935. The letter, in full (American Presidency Project, "Letter on the President's Ancestors"):

"My dear Mr. Slomovitz: I am grateful to you for your interesting letter of March 4th. I have no idea as to the source of the story which you say came from my old friend, Chase Osborn. All I know about the origin of the Roosevelt family in this country is that all branches bearing the name are apparently descended from Claes Martenssen Van Roosevelt, who came from Holland sometime before 1648—even the year is uncertain. In the dim distant past they may have been Jews or Catholics or Protestants. What I am more interested in is whether they were good citizens and believers in God. I hope they were both. Very sincerely yours, Franklin D. Roosevelt"

(Source: https://www.presidency.ucsb.edu/documents/letter-the-presidents-ancestors)

The New York Times printed the letter's text on its front page on March 15, 1935, before Slomovitz's own paper could run it — a national sensation at the time. Winrod's Revelator quoted the Times version as: "In the distant past my ancestors may have been Jews. All I know about the origin of the Roosevelt family is that they are apparently descended form Claes Martenszen van Roosevelt, who came from Holland." ("form" is the Revelator's typo for "from.")

The Eleanor "Jewish great-grandmother" letter (CLAIM, secondhand). Rabbi Stephen S. Wise wrote Slomovitz a "strictly personal and confidential" letter in late March 1935, reporting what his wife said Eleanor Roosevelt told her at a luncheon at the Wise home:

"MRS. Franklin D. Roosevelt said, 'Often Cousin Alice and I say that all the brains in the Roosevelt family come from our Jewish great-grandmother.' She added a name which, I recall it, was Esther Levy. Then she said, 'Whenever mention is made of our Jewish great-grandmother by Cousin Alice or myself, Franklin's mother gets very angry and says, "You know that is not so. Why do you say it?",' Mrs. Roosevelt spoke as with knowledge, conviction and authority… You must not, however, make use of this. I think it is best to let the matter die down now."

Slomovitz kept the letter private for ~45 years and published it in his book Purely Commentary (Wayne State University Press, 1981). Note what it is: a husband reporting his wife's report of a luncheon remark, about ELEANOR's side of the family joke ("Cousin Alice" is Alice Roosevelt Longworth, Theodore's daughter) — and the same letter records Sara Delano Roosevelt flatly denying it. It is not documentation of an ancestor. (Source — the letter as reprinted and discussed in Victor M. Bienstock, "Did FDR Really Have a Jewish Great-Grandmother?", Jewish Journal of Greater Miami / Jewish Floridian, February 5, 1982, via University of Florida digital collections: https://ufdcimages.uflib.ufl.edu/AA/00/01/00/90/02766/00177.txt)

Later echoes (CLAIMS). FDR's former son-in-law Curtis Bean Dall wrote in F.D.R.: My Exploited Father-in-Law (1967): "As I gathered it, the background of the Franklin Roosevelt family was a composite of English, Dutch, Jewish, and French stock." A claim in a memoir, offered without evidence.

2C. What the documented genealogy actually shows (RECORD)

  • The immigrant ancestor is Claes Martenszen van Rosenvelt. The Roosevelt Foundation at Oud-Vossemeer (the Dutch town that keeps the family's records) states: "Between 1650 and 1658 five of his children were baptized in the Reformed Dutch Church in New Amsterdam" — i.e., the family appears in the records as Dutch Reformed Christians from the first American generation onward. (Source: https://www.rooseveltoudvossemeer.nl/en/de-roosevelts/de-zeeuwse-roots-van-de-presidenten/)
  • The name is a Dutch place-name, not a changed Jewish name: land "known as the Rosevelt since 1481" lay between Poortvliet, Tholen and Oud-Vossemeer in Zeeland — "Rosevelt" = the rose field. Claes most likely came from the island of Tholen and took his byname from that farm/land, a common Dutch practice.
  • No baptismal, marriage, or synagogue record connects the family to Judaism at any point. Researcher Richard J. Garfunkel's summary of the evidence (2006): the name "was taken… from the Dutch translation of a hill or field of roses, not a Jewish sounding derivative name like Rosenvelt. The Martens were Dutch Protestants in New Amsterdam from the 1640's. There is no evidence that he or his forbearers were Jewish in Holland." (His headline conclusion: "Was the Roosevelt Branch Jewish? Probably Not!" — https://www.richardjgarfunkel.com/2006/12/06/was-fdr-from-jewish-roots-12-14-06/)
  • What IS true, and what fed the talk: FDR appointed more Jews to high office than any earlier president — a point even Winrod's Defender quoted from the Brooklyn Jewish Examiner of October 20, 1933: "The Roosevelt Administration has selected more Jews to fill influential positions than any previous administration." Appointments are documented; ancestry is not.

PART 3 — THE SAVED ITEMS: WHAT THEY ACTUALLY SHOW

M152 — IDENTIFIED (was: "book page, presidents of humble origin")

The quote on the photo (album item M152) —

"President Roosevelt is of Jewish descent, though not of the orthodox faith, his people having been converted to the Puritan and Pilgrim faith soon after they emigrated to the New World."

— is not from a book. It is from a syndicated newspaper feature titled "As Paris Sees Us" ("We're a Queer Sort Folk — What Parisian Papers Print"), which quoted French newspapers' garbled America stories for American readers to laugh at. Verified verbatim in the Polk County Observer (Monmouth, Polk County, Oregon), Friday, June 5, 1914, page 6 (Image 6), via the University of Oregon's Historic Oregon Newspapers (the same scan Chronicling America carries; LOC itself was blocked tonight): https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn96088088/1914-06-05/ed-1/seq-6.pdf

The feature's presidents passage, re-assembled from the page's interleaved OCR columns (the page prints three columns; OCR mixes them, but the sentences are complete and in order):

"The truly democratic origin of our great men is the subject for another article. It tells us: 'The presidents of the great American republic have all been of humble origin. The great Lincoln was a tailor and worked in great poverty for many years prior to his taking his place as president. President Cleveland was a New Jersey farmer and was perhaps the most prosperous of all the presidents in his early life. After he had served several terms he returned to his farm at Prince's Town and continued to lead the life of a farmer until he died, doing much of the work with his own hands because he liked to. "President Roosevelt is of Jewish descent, though not of the orthodox faith, his people having been converted to the Puritan and pilgrim faith soon after they emigrated to the new world. Mr. Roosevelt was a policeman for many years before he entered political life and was also a midshipman in the navy for a time. He has always plainly shown his humble origin and during his terms at the White House would take long vacations and join parties of…'"

Read it for what it is: a PARIS writer's error-filled sketch (Lincoln was not a tailor — that was Andrew Johnson; Cleveland was not a farmer; "midshipman in the navy" fits nobody), reprinted in Oregon as a joke about French ignorance. The Roosevelt meant is Theodore (the policeman = Police Commissioner of New York, 1895–97 — the Paris man shrank the commissioner into a "policeman"). So M152 ties both halves of the claim (a) and (b) to a single page. Whether the photograph is this exact Oregon paper or another paper running the same syndicated feature cannot be proved from the photo alone — but the wording is a word-for-word match, so the feature itself is identified.
