Baron Hirsch — Chronicling America finds (Library of Congress)

Search run 2026-11-19 on loc.gov ("Baron Hirsch" and "Baron de Hirsch," 1890–1897). The archive holds a lot on him — 1,863 pages contain "baron hirsch" overall; 416 exact-phrase "Baron de Hirsch" hits in 1890–1897 alone. Six pages verified readable, covering the fund, the colonization plan, his death, and the Argentina colonies.

Note on amounts: the papers do not agree. The New-York Tribune and The Journal print the U.S. fund as $2,500,000; Foster's Daily Democrat prints $2,400,000. Each paper is quoted as printed.

1. The 1891 fund — New-York Tribune (New York, N.Y.), February 8, 1891, front page

Headline: "TO AID HEBREW IMMIGRANTS. A LARGE SUM DEVOTED TO THIS PURPOSE. BARON HIRSCH AUTHORIZES THE USE OF $2,500,000 TO ESTABLISH HEBREWS ON FARM LANDS."

"Baron Hirsch, the wealthy associate of the Prince of Wales, has telegraphed Jesse Seligman, treasurer of the Baron Hirsch Fund, that the trustees of the fund may draw upon him for $2,500,000, to be applied to the relief of the Hebrew immigrants to this country."

"We do not only give them the money they need to start afresh in this country, but we buy farms, tools, and everything that is necessary to put them on their feet, so that they can make a good and independent living."

https://www.loc.gov/resource/sn83030214/1891-02-08/ed-1/?sp=1&q=baron+hirsch

2. The colonization plan — The Portland Daily Press (Portland, Me.), July 30, 1892

Headline: "A GRAND SCHEME — How Baron Hirsch Proposes to Rescue the Jews of Russia" (reprinted from the New York Sun).

"The project is one of surpassing magnitude and of an extraordinary character."

"From the fact, however, that he has purchased broad tracts of land in the Argentine Republic, and other tracts in South Africa, and has disbursed great sums of money for the service of Jewish immigrants to the United States…"

The article describes a proposed $100,000,000 scheme for the organized emigration of 3,500,000 Jews. https://www.loc.gov/resource/sn83016025/1892-07-30/ed-1/?sp=3&q=baron+hirsch

3. Death — Foster's Daily Democrat (Dover, N.H.), April 22, 1896

Headline: "LIFE WORK ENDED. Baron Hirsch Dies Suddenly at His Estate Near Komorn. WAS A VICTIM OF HEART DISEASE"

"Vienna, April 21.—Baron Hirsch died this morning of heart disease at his estate near Komorn"

"he made an offer of $10,000,000 to the Russian government for schools, with the one proviso that no distinction should be made as to race or religion."

This paper gives the U.S. fund as $2,400,000, "to be paid in monthly instalments of $10,000." https://www.loc.gov/resource/sn84023058/1896-04-22/ed-1/?sp=3&q=baron+hirsch

4. Death and the fund — The Journal (New York, N.Y.), April 22, 1896

Headline: "JEWS LOSE THEIR GREAT BENEFACTOR, Baron de Hirsch Dies of Apoplexy in Austria." Sub-article: "BARON HIRSCH'S FUND. Important Work the Dead Philanthropist Has Done for His American Co-Religionists."

"Maurice Baron de Hirsch, universally known as the 'Millionaire Moses,' died of apoplexy in Austria to-day."

"The Russian Government in 1892 agreed to the scheme of emigration of the Jews to the Argentine and Canada and other countries, the Baron guaranteeing to expend $10,000,000 on the work. The colonies established in the Argentine were recently reported to be in a flourishing condition."

This paper dates the U.S. fund to March 1890 and puts it at $2,500,000. https://www.loc.gov/resource/sn84031792/1896-04-22/ed-1/?sp=3&q=baron+hirsch

5. The New Jersey colony — Pittsburg Dispatch (Pittsburg, Pa.), June 19, 1892

Headline: "A FUGITIVE COLONY — Baron Hirsch's Plan for the Care of Russian Refugees in New Jersey."

"The reported advent in Pittsburg and Western Pennsylvania of Russian Hebrews from Baron Hirsch's Argentine Republic Colony, makes especially interesting his little settlement in New Jersey."

"It is now nearly a year since the first and as yet the only American colony of Russian refugees settled under the auspices of the Baron Hirsch fund began its existence at Woodbine, N.J."

https://www.loc.gov/resource/sn84024546/1892-06-19/ed-1/?sp=24&q=baron+hirsch

6. The Argentina colonies — Bill Barlow's Budget (Douglas, Wyo.), May 15, 1895

Headline: "BARON HIRSCH'S COLONIES. Those in South America May Now Apparently Be Regarded Successful."

"The report for 1894 of the Jewish Colonization Association shows that the Jewish colonies in the Argentine are gradually but surely passing the experimental stage."

"Before this year four colonies were established, having a total area of 200,000 acres, occupied by 563 families."

The report also says nearly every family had received 185 acres, and that the association had spent $1,851,545 since its foundation, $900,000 of it written off as lost. https://www.loc.gov/resource/sn92067125/1895-05-15/ed-1/?sp=7&q=baron+hirsch+argentine
