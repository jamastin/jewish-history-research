A personal research project into Jewish history, told from the original newspapers and documents — exact words from the original pages, not summaries.

Starter site: `index.html` plus topic pages on the 1878 Greenback find, Patton & the O'Donnell column of 1945, the Nazi records and population counts, Hitler and the other major figures, Baron Hirsch and the colonies, and the menorah and Rome.
