Where Did We Go Wrong

Jewish history research — from the original newspapers and documents

This is a personal research project with one rule: go to the original source and read its exact words — the newspaper page as it was printed, the document as it was written — not summaries, not snippets, not somebody else's retelling.

The collection holds 25 topic threads and 407 catalogued items, most of them pages clipped from old newspapers, each one hunted back to its original source and checked. Below are some of the threads, with a few of the finished pieces to read.

The Greenback Find — 1878
A sentence remembered from an old clipping — “To be slaves to gold is to be slaves to the Rothchilds and German Jews” — hunted through the archives and found, word for word, in a Minnesota newspaper of April 4, 1878.
 Patton & the O'Donnell Column — 1945
A New York columnist blamed General Patton's removal on Jewish leaders in Washington. The papers answered him, and within three weeks he printed a retraction: “On the evidence, our statements in Capitol Stuff were untrue.”
 The Numbers — Nazi Records & Population Counts
How the Holocaust death toll is actually built: the killers' own tallies, two SS documents that match to the exact digit, camp-by-camp transport records, and the population counts of 1933 and 1950.
 Hitler & the Other Figures
Hitler's writings and reading, and a page each for Himmler, Heydrich, Eichmann, Göring, Goebbels and Höss — their key documents, and what the newspapers of the time said about each man.
 Baron Hirsch & the Colonies
The railroad baron who spent a fortune settling Russian Jews as farmers in Argentina — and the four colonies of the 1894 report, identified.
 The Menorah & Rome
The seven-branched lamp of the Temple: what it was, what it meant, and its trail from Jerusalem to the Arch of Titus in Rome.
